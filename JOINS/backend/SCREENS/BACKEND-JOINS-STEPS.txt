ART 14 — What we will demonstrate in React
This gives us an excellent frontend teaching structure later:
PostgreSQL JOIN Demonstrations
│
├── INNER JOIN
├── LEFT JOIN
├── RIGHT JOIN
├── FULL OUTER JOIN
├── CROSS JOIN
├── NATURAL JOIN
├── SELF JOIN
│
├── Employees Without Department
├── Departments Without Employees
│
├── Employee → Project
└── Employee → Department → Project

For example, your INNER JOIN card can show:
INNER JOIN
────────────────────────────────────

Employee             Department
John Doe             Development
Jane Smith           Marketing
Bob Johnson          Development
Alice Williams       Sales
Charlie Brown        Development
Eva Davis            Marketing
--------------------------------------------
And your LEFT JOIN will visibly demonstrate the NULL department for Elena and Victor. That's exactly the behavior your supplied SQL is intended to demonstrate.   left_join_right_join
The RIGHT JOIN will similarly make Technical Support appear with a NULL employee.   left_join_right_join
The FULL OUTER JOIN will bring both unmatched sides together.   full_outer_join
And the CROSS JOIN will be particularly nice visually because every employee is paired with every department.
